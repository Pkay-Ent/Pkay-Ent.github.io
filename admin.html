Tutorials

Forex signals

Newest first. When a trade closes, open it and change the status to Win, Loss or Breakeven, then publish. Post losses too, because it builds trust.

Earn online section

Add or edit ways to make money online, or offer your own PDF guides with a price tag. Visitors with a price see a "Buy on WhatsApp" button.

Important: everything uploaded to your site is public, and anyone with the link can download it. For a paid guide, upload only a short free preview PDF, then send the full PDF on WhatsApp after the client pays. Leave the price empty (or write Free) for free guides.

Payment details

Leave a box empty to hide that method. A "Ways to pay" section appears on your site once at least one is filled. Only enter addresses and IDs that receive money. Never type a seed phrase, private key or password anywhere here. After pasting an address, double-check every character.

Site notice

A short banner shown at the very top of your site (for example "Open for new orders this week"). Leave it empty to hide it.

Publish settings

These let this page send your changes straight to your GitHub site. They are saved only in this browser. Use a private phone or PC.

Backup and manual options

No token? Click "Download content.json", then on GitHub open your repository, choose Add file, then Upload files, drop the file in and commit. Your site updates within a minute or two.
